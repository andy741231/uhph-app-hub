Research Cores

Four cores. One pipeline from idea to impact.

Each core handles a distinct part of the research-to-impact pipeline. Together, they provide everything an investigator needs — from study design to community partnership to dissemination.

Core 01

Administrative Core

Stewardship for Real-World Impact

Contact This Core

The Administrative Core builds and sustains the organizational infrastructure capable of long-term scientific impact. We handle governance, evaluation, strategic direction, and the operational systems that make everything else possible.

What we provide

Governance

Internal advisory boards, external review, and compliance oversight.

Evaluation

Quarterly metrics tracking with public dashboard reporting.

Strategic Direction

Annual planning cycles aligned with community priorities.

Operations

Financial management, reporting, and NIH compliance.

Core 02

Investigator Development

Growing Tomorrow's Research Leaders

Contact This Core

We focus on people, not committees. Through mentoring, pilot funding, and structured career pathways, we help early-stage investigators build the skills, networks, and track records they need to secure independent funding.

Mentoring

One-on-one mentoring with senior investigators across disciplines.

Pilot Awards

Competitive pilot funding up to $50K for preliminary data collection.

Career Pathways

Structured programs from K-award preparation to R01 submission.

Success Stories

24 pilot projects funded; 12 investigators have secured independent funding.
