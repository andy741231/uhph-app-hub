Investigator Journey

From "I have an idea" to real-world impact.

Eight steps. Every step links directly to the service that moves you forward. The journey isn't always linear — but every step has a door, and we make sure the doors are open.

  1. 01

    I have an idea

    Start with a research question about chronic disease in your community. We'll help you refine it into a fundable hypothesis.

    Start Here
  2. 02

    Need collaborators

    Connect with clinicians, biostatisticians, and community partners through our investigator network.

    Network
  3. 03

    Need consultation

    Get study design, methods, and navigation support from the Research Capacity Core. 189 consultations completed to date.

    Research Capacity Core
  4. 04

    Need pilot funding

    Apply for pilot awards through the Investigator Development Core. Up to $50K for preliminary data collection.

    Investigator Dev Core
  5. 05

    Need community partners

    Engage organizations through the Community Engagement Core. 31 active partnerships across 17 counties.

    Community Engagement Core
  6. 06

    Need implementation support

    Translate findings into practice with implementation science expertise from the Research Capacity Core.

    Research Capacity Core
  7. 07

    Need dissemination

    Publish, present, and share results with both academic and community audiences. 156 publications to date.

    All Cores
  8. 08

    Need evaluation

    Measure impact and feed lessons back into the next research question. The dashboard tracks it publicly.

    Administrative Core

Ready to start your journey?

Tell us where you are in the process and we'll connect you with the right core and the right people.
