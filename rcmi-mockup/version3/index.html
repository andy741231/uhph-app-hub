Featured Case Study

Coaching Diabetes in East Houston

A pragmatic trial of community health coaches reduced A1c by 1.4 points on average in a low-resource neighborhood — and is now scaling to three additional ZIP codes.

Chronic Disease Health Coaching

A scalable, low-cost model that works where clinics can't reach.

Community health coaches, trained and supervised by RCMI investigators, delivered a 12-week diabetes self-management program in participants' homes. The result: a 1.4-point average A1c reduction, sustained at 6-month follow-up, at a fraction of the cost of clinic-based interventions.
