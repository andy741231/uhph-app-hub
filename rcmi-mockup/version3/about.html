About RCMI

Turning research into better health for Houston and beyond.

The Research Centers in Minority Institutions (RCMI) program at the University of Houston exists to close the chronic-disease gap — building research capacity, developing investigators, and partnering with communities across the Gulf Coast region.

Our Mission

Three things, done well.

Research Capacity

We build the infrastructure, tools, methods, and expertise that make rigorous chronic-disease research possible — from study design to evidence synthesis.

Investigator Development

We mentor early-stage investigators, fund pilot projects, and clear the path from idea to impact through structured career pathways.

Community Partnership

We work side-by-side with communities so research reflects real priorities and produces real-world change — not just publications.

Our Values

What guides our work.

Rigor

Every project meets the highest scientific standards. We measure impact, not just activity.

Equity

Chronic disease doesn't affect communities equally. Neither should access to research infrastructure.

Partnership

We don't study communities. We work with them. The research question comes from the neighborhood.

Sustainability

We build capacity that lasts beyond the funding cycle — in people, institutions, and communities.

Transparency

Our dashboard is public. Our metrics are sourced. If the numbers stop moving, we say so.

Impact

The question isn't "did we publish?" The question is "did it change anything?"
