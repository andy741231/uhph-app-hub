Research in Action

Every story answers four questions.

What problem? What did we learn? Why does it matter? What's next?

Diabetes

Reaching patients before the diagnosis does

  • Problem: Late-stage diabetes diagnoses in underserved zip codes.
  • Learned: Community health workers doubled early screening rates.
  • Why it matters: Earlier intervention prevents costly complications.
  • What's next: Scaling the model to three additional counties.
Cardiovascular

A pharmacy-led model for blood pressure control

  • Problem: Uncontrolled hypertension among working-age adults.
  • Learned: Pharmacist-led coaching improved control at 6 months.
  • Why it matters: A scalable, low-cost path to fewer cardiac events.
  • What's next: A larger pragmatic trial across two health systems.
Survivorship

Rebuilding trust after cancer treatment ends

  • Problem: Survivors falling out of follow-up care.
  • Learned: Peer navigators re-engaged 3 in 4 lapsed patients.
  • Why it matters: Continuity of care improves long-term survival.
  • What's next: Training more peer navigators community-wide.
Mental Health

Meeting families where they already gather

  • Problem: Low uptake of behavioral health services.
  • Learned: Faith-based settings tripled program enrollment.
  • Why it matters: Trust travels faster than clinic referrals.
  • What's next: A toolkit for other congregations to adopt.
Maternal Health

Closing the postpartum follow-up gap

  • Problem: Missed postpartum visits among high-risk mothers.
  • Learned: Text-based reminders raised attendance by 28%.
  • Why it matters: Postpartum care catches complications early.
  • What's next: Integrating reminders into standard discharge.
Access

Mobile clinics as research infrastructure

  • Problem: Rural counties with no nearby specialty care.
  • Learned: Mobile units enabled recruitment at 4x prior rates.
  • Why it matters: Representative research requires reachable research.
  • What's next: A second mobile unit funded for 2027.
