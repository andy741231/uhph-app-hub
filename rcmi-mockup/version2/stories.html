Coaching Diabetes in East Houston

What problem?
Uncontrolled type 2 diabetes in a low-resource neighborhood.
What did we learn?
Community health coaches reduced A1c by 1.4 points on average.
Why does it matter?
It's a scalable, low-cost model that works where clinics can't reach.
What's next?
Expanding to three additional ZIP codes in 2026.

By Dr. Maria Santos · East Houston · 2026

Implementing BP Pathways

What problem?
Hypertension control lagged in community clinics.
What did we learn?
Standardized pathways plus weekly data review improved control by 18%.
Why does it matter?
Implementation science can move the needle in real clinics.
What's next?
Packaging the pathway for statewide adoption.

By Dr. James Chen · Harris County · 2025

Listening to Older Adults

What problem?
Chronic disease isolation among seniors in Fort Bend County.
What did we learn?
Peer-led discussion groups reduced loneliness and improved self-management.
Why does it matter?
It reframes isolation as a treatable chronic-disease risk factor.
What's next?
Training 20 new peer leaders across the region.

By Dr. Aisha Williams · Fort Bend · 2026

Mobile Clinics, Real Data

What problem?
Rural counties lacked consistent chronic-disease screening.
What did we learn?
Mobile clinics with real-time data capture tripled screening rates.
Why does it matter?
It proves you can bring research infrastructure to the patient.
What's next?
Two new mobile units deploying in 2026.

By Dr. Robert Lee · Rural TX · 2025

Food as Medicine

What problem?
Food insecurity driving poor diabetes outcomes.
What did we learn?
"Food pharmacy" prescriptions reduced ER visits by 22%.
Why does it matter?
It connects the clinic to the food system — and measures it.
What's next?
Scaling to 12 clinics across the Gulf Coast.

By Dr. Priya Patel · Houston · 2026

Walking Groups, Walking Data

What problem?
Sedentary behavior and social isolation in older adults.
What did we learn?
Structured walking groups improved both BP and loneliness scores.
Why does it matter?
It's a free, scalable intervention with measurable clinical impact.
What's next?
Standardizing the protocol for any community org to adopt.

By Dr. Kevin Nguyen · Houston · 2025
