## 10. Visual Identity

- Follow the [University of Houston Brand Style Guide](https://www.uh.edu/brand/brand-identity/style-guide/index.php).
- Theme colors:
  - Primary red: `#C8102E`
  - White: `#FFFFFF`
  - Secondary: Black `#000000`, Slate `#54585A`
  - Accent (choose one): Brick `#960C22`, Chocolate `#640817`, Cream `#FFF9D9`, Gray `#888B8D`, Gold `#F6BE00`, Mustard `#D89B00`, Ocher `#B97800`, Teal `#00B388`, Green `#00866C`, Forest `#005950`
- Fonts:
  - Headlines: League Gothic
  - Subheads and body: Source Sans 3 (via [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Source+Sans+3))
  - Supporting body copy: Crimson
- Consistent graphics throughout.
- Every page uses the same icon language.
- Simple illustrations.
- Lots of breathing room.
- Very few stock photos.
- Professional photography whenever possible.
