Exercise & Cancer Prevention

Investigating the long-term effects of moderate cardio on cancer risk reduction in adults aged 30-50.

Duration: 5 Years
Commitment: 60 mins/week
Location: Remote / Local Gym
Compensation: $50/month

About This Study

This research aims to understand the correlation between regular moderate cardiovascular exercise and the reduction of specific cancer markers in the bloodstream. We are partnering with major health organizations to conduct this longitudinal study.

Participants will be asked to follow a prescribed exercise regimen and log their activities using a provided wearable device. Quarterly blood tests will be required at a local partner lab (Quest or LabCorp).

Eligibility Criteria

  • Age between 30 and 50 years old.
  • No prior history of cancer.
  • Currently exercising less than 30 minutes per week.
  • Willing to wear a fitness tracker (provided).
  • Resident of the United States.
